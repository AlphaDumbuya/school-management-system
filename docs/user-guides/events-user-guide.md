## Introduction

The Events Management system allows you to discover, register for, and participate in school events. Whether it's academic events, sports competitions, cultural activities, or parent meetings, you can manage all your event participation in one place.

### Who Can Use This Module?

- **Students**: Browse and register for events
- **Parents**: Register for parent meetings and view student event participation
- **Teachers**: View events, register, and manage class events
- **Administrators**: Full access to all event features

---

## Viewing Events

### Accessing the Events Page

1. Navigate to **Events** from the main menu
2. The events page displays all published events you have access to

### Understanding Event Cards

Each event displays:
- **Event Title** and **Description**
- **Date and Time**
- **Location** (physical or virtual link)
- **Event Type** badge (Academic, Sports, Cultural, etc.)
- **Capacity** information (Available spots / Total capacity)
- **Status** badge (Published, Cancelled, etc.)

### Filtering Events

You can filter events by:

**By Status:**
- Published
- Upcoming
- Past

**By Type:**
- Academic
- Sports
- Cultural
- Parent Meeting
- Exam
- Other

**By Date Range:**
- Use the calendar picker to select a date range
- View events for specific months or weeks

**Search:**
- Enter keywords to search event titles and descriptions
- Example: "science fair", "sports day", "parent meeting"

### Event Details

Click on any event card to view full details:

- Complete description
- Start and end times
- Location with map (if available)
- Organizer information
- List of attendees (if public)
- Registration requirements
- Capacity status
- Related files or resources

## Event Registration

### How to Register for an Event

1. **Find the Event**
   - Browse or search for the event you want to attend
   - Click on the event card to view details

2. **Review Event Information**
   - Check the date, time, and location
   - Review capacity and requirements
   - Check if approval is required

3. **Click Register**
   - Click the **Register** button
   - Choose your RSVP status:
     - **Going**: You will definitely attend
     - **Maybe**: You're interested but not confirmed
     - **Not Going**: You won't attend (for tracking purposes)

4. **Additional Information**
   - **Guest Count**: If guests are allowed, specify how many
   - **Custom Fields**: Fill in any required registration form fields
   - **Notes**: Add any special requests or notes

5. **Confirmation**
   - You'll receive a confirmation message
   - Email/SMS notification will be sent (if enabled)
   - Event appears in your "My Events" list

### Registration Statuses

- **Confirmed**: Your registration is approved
- **Pending**: Waiting for organizer approval
- **Waitlisted**: Event is full, you're on the waitlist
- **Cancelled**: You cancelled your registration

### Capacity and Waitlist

**When an Event is Full:**
- If waitlist is enabled, you can join the waitlist
- You'll be notified if a spot becomes available
- Waitlist positions are first-come, first-served

**When a Spot Opens:**
- First person on waitlist is automatically promoted
- You'll receive immediate notification
- You have 24 hours to confirm (or next person is selected)

## Managing Your Registrations

### Viewing Your Events

Access **My Events** to see:

**Upcoming Events**
- Events you're registered for
- Date, time, and location
- Your RSVP status
- Quick actions (Update RSVP, Cancel)

**Past Events**
- Events you attended
- Attendance status
- Event feedback/reviews

### Updating Your RSVP

1. Go to **My Events**
2. Find the event
3. Click **Update RSVP**
4. Change your status (Going → Maybe, etc.)
5. Update guest count if needed
6. Save changes

### Cancelling Registration

1. Find the event in **My Events**
2. Click **Cancel Registration**
3. Confirm cancellation
4. Optional: Provide a reason

**Note:** Cancellation policies may vary by event. Some events may have deadlines for cancellation.

### Notifications

You'll receive notifications for:

- **Registration Confirmation**: Immediate
- **Event Reminders**:
  - 24 hours before event
  - 1 hour before event (for upcoming events)
- **Status Changes**: When approval status changes
- **Event Updates**: When event details change
- **Cancellations**: If event is cancelled

**Notification Channels:**
- In-app notifications
- Email
- SMS (if enabled)
- Push notifications (mobile app)

## Attendance & Check-In

### QR Code Check-In

**For In-Person Events:**

1. **Arrive at Event**
   - Go to check-in desk/station

2. **Show Your QR Code**
   - Open event in app
   - Tap "Check-In"
   - Show QR code to staff

3. **Scan Confirmation**
   - Staff scans your QR code
   - Instant check-in confirmation
   - Attendance automatically recorded

**Generating Your QR Code:**
- Open event details
- Tap **Check-In QR Code**
- QR code is generated instantly
- Valid for the event duration
- Can be saved to phone wallet

### Manual Check-In

If QR code is unavailable:

1. Provide your name/ID to staff
2. Staff marks you as present
3. Confirmation displayed on screen

### Check-Out

For events tracking duration:

1. Tap **Check Out** when leaving
2. System calculates your attendance duration
3. Important for events with minimum attendance requirements

### Attendance Reports

**View Your Attendance History:**

1. Go to **Profile** → **Event Attendance**
2. See all events you attended
3. Filter by date range or event type
4. View attendance statistics:
   - Total events attended
   - Attendance rate
   - No-show count
   - Average duration

**Attendance Matters:**
- Some events may have minimum attendance requirements
- Attendance history may be visible to administrators
- Good attendance may be rewarded or required for certain privileges

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Can I register for multiple events at the same time?**  
A: Yes, but check for scheduling conflicts. The system will warn you if events overlap.

**Q: What if I can't attend an event I registered for?**  
A: Cancel your registration as soon as possible to free up the spot for others.

**Q: How do I know if my registration needs approval?**  
A: The event details will clearly state "Approval Required." You'll see "Pending" status after registering.

**Q: Can I bring guests to an event?**  
A: Only if "Guests Allowed" is indicated on the event page. Specify guest count during registration.

### Registration Issues

**Q: The event shows as "Full" but I need to attend. What can I do?**  
A: Join the waitlist if available. You'll be notified if a spot opens up.

**Q: I registered but didn't receive a confirmation email**  
A: Check your spam folder. If not there, contact the event organizer or check "My Events" to verify registration.

**Q: Can I transfer my registration to someone else?**  
A: No, registrations are non-transferable. Cancel and have the other person register.

**Q: The registration form asks for information I don't want to provide**  
A: Contact the event organizer. Some fields may be optional or can be discussed privately.

### Technical Issues

**Q: The QR code isn't working at check-in**  
A: Ensure your phone screen brightness is at maximum. If still not working, use manual check-in with staff.

**Q: I can't find an event I know exists**  
A: Check your filters. Some events may be restricted by role or grade level.

**Q: The app says I'm already registered but I don't remember registering**  
A: Check your registration history in "My Events." Contact support if you believe it's an error.

### Event Changes

**Q: What happens if an event is cancelled?**  
A: You'll be notified immediately via all enabled channels. Any fees will be refunded if applicable.

**Q: Can I suggest changes to an event?**  
A: Yes, contact the event organizer directly. Their contact info is on the event details page.

**Q: How often is the event calendar updated?**  
A: Events are updated in real-time. Refresh the page to see the latest changes.

### Privacy & Notifications

**Q: Who can see that I registered for an event?**  
A: Event organizers and administrators can see registrations. Other attendees may see your name if the attendee list is public.

**Q: How do I turn off event notifications?**  
A: Go to **Settings** → **Notifications** → **Events** and customize your preferences.

**Q: Can I make my event attendance private?**  
A: Contact your school administrator about privacy settings. Some information may be required for school records.
